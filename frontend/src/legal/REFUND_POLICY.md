# FlowSense Refund Policy

**Version:** 2026-10-24  
**Effective date:** October 24, 2026  
**Status:** No checkout or payment processing exists in this FlowSense deployment.

FlowSense currently has no paid plan, online checkout, or payment collection feature. No purchase is made through this application, so this application cannot currently accept or process a refund request. This does not waive any rights a customer may have under applicable law or a separate agreement.

Before offering a paid product, the operator must identify the seller, publish the total price and renewal terms before purchase, provide a usable cancellation path, explain refund eligibility and timing, provide a monitored billing contact, and honor any mandatory state or federal cancellation and refund rights. The federal FTC Mail, Internet, or Telephone Order Merchandise Rule addresses shipment timing and refunds for delayed merchandise; it is not a general five-business-day cancellation right for this software. No Colombia-specific five-day withdrawal promise is made for this U.S.-focused deployment. Obtain legal review for each market and product before enabling checkout.
