## 2. Authorized use and source permissions

Use the workspace only for lawful mobility observation and review. You must have authority to connect each camera, video stream, road dataset, and other source you submit. Follow the source provider’s conditions, keep required attribution visible, and do not bypass access controls or use credentials in stream URLs. In particular, public availability of a Caltrans feed does not itself grant permission for commercial redistribution, recording, or analysis. Obtain any required Caltrans/CHP agreement or written authorization before those activities.

Do not use FlowSense to identify a person, read a license plate, make a law-enforcement decision, control traffic signals, or automatically dispatch personnel. Do not rely on a model candidate or one camera frame as confirmation of an incident. A human operator must review the source, timestamp, coverage, and uncertainty.

## 3. Data and availability

Public video and connected sources may be unavailable, delayed, changed, or inaccurate. Camera-analysis sessions can save timestamped observations and short video evidence clips for later review; see the [Privacy Notice](/legal/privacy) for the repository’s default storage and cleanup behavior. A camera’s proximity does not establish that it covers a road. Detection counts are limited to visible frames and reviewed zones; they are not calibrated speed, whole-road occupancy, or a guarantee of conditions. The service and third-party sources may change or stop without notice.
