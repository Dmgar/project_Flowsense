# Product Brief

## One-Sentence Product

FlowSense converts urban camera feeds into a real-time mobility intelligence layer for city teams.

## The Pain

Cities often have many cameras, but limited structured understanding of what those cameras show. Operators may know that traffic is bad, but not which corridor is getting worse, what type of movement is causing it, whether pedestrians are overflowing, where freight is adding pressure, or which issue deserves attention first.

## Primary Users

- Mobility operations manager: monitors the city and assigns reviews.
- Traffic operations analyst: diagnoses corridors, signal timing, queue buildup, and bottlenecks.
- Road safety analyst: reviews near-miss patterns, pedestrian pressure, and risky movement.
- Field operations coordinator: receives issues that need verification.
- Emergency operations liaison: uses road intelligence for route confidence and backup corridors.
- Urban planning team: reviews before/after evidence for interventions.

## What FlowSense Improves

- Visibility: turns camera feeds into live movement metrics.
- Prioritization: ranks hotspots and risk signals by impact, confidence, severity, and evidence.
- Diagnosis: explains why a corridor or intersection is under pressure.
- Review workflow: lets humans inspect camera evidence before action.
- Measurement: supports before/after evaluation after signal, curb, lane, or safety interventions.

## What FlowSense Does Not Improve By Itself

- It does not remove congestion without an operational response.
- It does not automatically control traffic lights unless an integration exists.
- It does not prove citywide impact without pilots and before/after metrics.
- It does not replace urban planning, public transport policy, or infrastructure investment.
- It should not be positioned as individual surveillance.

## Best Hackathon Positioning

Use this framing:

> FlowSense helps cities turn existing cameras into actionable mobility intelligence: live conditions, corridor diagnosis, pedestrian pressure, risk signals, and human-reviewed operational actions.

Avoid this framing:

> FlowSense is an AI that detects cars and routes ambulances.

The stronger story is citywide operations, with emergency response as one high-impact application.
